Gravitational lens

Singular isothermal sphere in front of a field of background galaxies

Lens equation

β = θ − θE θ̂
Each source at β has an image at |θ| = |β| + θE, stretched tangentially by θ/β. Sources inside the Einstein radius θE get a second, fainter image on the other side.

Strong → weak

Close to the lens: arcs and, for exact alignment, an Einstein ring. Far away the shear γ = θE/2θ is only a few percent, smaller than the galaxies' own random shapes. Weak lensing measures it by averaging many galaxies.
